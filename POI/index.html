The Core Concept

What Is a Point of Interest (POI)?

1
A Planned Reaction Zone
A POI is a specific price zone marked on your chart where price is expected to react or pivot.
🚏
The Bus Stop Rule
You don't chase a bus down the highway. You wait at the bus stop. The POI is your bus stop!
🎯
No POI = No Action
If price is in the middle of nowhere, do nothing. Only pay attention when price arrives at your zone.
Simple Idea: Mark your zone in advance → Let price come to you.
POI Zone (Planned Reaction)
No-Man's Land (Random Movement)

What is a POI? 04
POI #1 • Foundations

SNR: Support & Resistance

R
Resistance = Ceiling 🛑
Price reaches the ceiling and turns down. Selling pressure halts the advance.
S
Support = Floor 🟢
Price reaches the floor and rebounds. Buying interest halts the decline.
Core Principle: Price reacts at established boundaries (Floors & Ceilings), not in the middle!
Resistance (Price Rejection)
Support (Price Bounce)

POI #2 • Bullish Setup

RBS: Resistance Becomes Support

1
Ceiling Rejects Price
Price hits resistance ceiling and pulls back.
2
Breakout! (BOS)
Big green candle blows right through the ceiling.
3
3. Retest & Reaction
Old ceiling is tested as a new Floor. Price confirms support with a bounce!
Core Concept: Once broken, previous resistance inverts its role to act as future support.
Broken Ceiling → New Floor

POI #4 • The Liquidity Magnet

FVG: Fair Value Gap (The 3-Candle Gap)

1
The 3-Candle Gap
Candle 1 wick and Candle 3 wick do NOT touch.
2
The Magnet Effect 🧲
Price hates empty space. It pulls back to fill the gap.
3
Tap & Bounce!
Price touches the gap (or 50% midpoint) and continues the trend.
Core Concept: Rapid momentum creates an imbalance; the market returns to rebalance efficient pricing.
FVG (Unfilled Gap)
Price Fills & Bounces

POI #5 • Fibonacci Discount

OTE: Optimal Trade Entry

1
Measure the Swing
Pull Fibonacci from Swing Low (0%) to Swing High (100%).
2
Above 50% = Premium Zone
Price above 50% is Premium (expensive). Price below 50% is Discount (cheap).
3
70.5% = OTE Sweet Spot 🎯
The 0.705 Fibonacci ratio marks the institutional deep discount retracement level.
Core Concept: The 0.62 to 0.79 zone identifies the highest-probability pullback area in a trend.
70.5% Discount Sweet Spot
0% Swing Low Baseline

Mentee Discipline

4 Simple Rules for Mentees

01
Higher Timeframe Trend

Align analysis with the higher timeframe direction. POIs aligned with the broader market trend have greater reliability.

02
Never Chase Price

If price leaves without touching your POI, let it go. Missing a trade costs $0; chasing costs your account.

03
Wait for Rejection

Don't enter blindly. Wait for a wick rejection candle to prove buyers/sellers are reacting at your POI.

04
Risk & Invalidation

Every level can fail. Understand the exact point where a POI is invalidated, and always practice on demo first.

Mentor Mantra: "Professional traders protect capital first, take profits second."
