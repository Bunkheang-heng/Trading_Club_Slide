# Point of Interest (POI) — OTE, FVG, SNR, SBR & RBS

An ultra-clean, visual-first presentation slide deck teaching **Point of Interest (POI)** for mentees in the **AUPP Trading Club** (American University of Phnom Penh).

## Overview
Designed to be simple, punchy, and beginner-friendly with minimal text and maximum visual clarity:
- **Zero Walls of Text**: Bullet points are concise 1-liners with bold key terms and clear explanations.
- **The Bus Stop Rule**: Never enter in the middle of nowhere; wait at the POI bus stop.
- **1. SNR (Support & Resistance)**: Resistance = Ceiling 🛑 (Rejection) • Support = Floor 🟢 (Bounce).
- **2. RBS (Resistance Becomes Support)**: Ceiling breaks $\rightarrow$ inverts to new Floor (Support Confirmation).
- **3. SBR (Support Becomes Resistance)**: Floor breaks $\rightarrow$ inverts to new Ceiling (Resistance Confirmation).
- **4. FVG (Fair Value Gap)**: The 3-candle imbalance where price returns to rebalance liquidity.
- **5. OTE (Optimal Trade Entry)**: The 70.5% institutional discount retracement sweet spot 🎯.
- **Interactive Practice**: Clickable buttons for mentors to test mentees live in class.
- **4 Golden Rules**: Higher timeframe trend, patience, confirmation wicks, and invalidation awareness.
